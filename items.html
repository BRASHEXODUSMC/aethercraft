MOB ENCYCLOPEDIA

Minecraft Mobs

Expanded mob encyclopedia covering passive, neutral, hostile, tameable and boss mobs, with health, hearts, interaction/taming and breeding food where applicable. Mobs do not use the player hunger bar, so food fields describe taming, healing, interaction or breeding instead. Renders load over HTTPS and gracefully fall back if the external renderer is unavailable.

Catalog note: Minecraft changes over time. This template includes a broad 1.21.x-oriented catalog generated from the major block and item families. Server owners can edit data/items.json to add modded, custom, future, or server-exclusive entries.
