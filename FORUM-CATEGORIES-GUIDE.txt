CREATING A TOPIC
----------------
1. Log in.
2. Open forums.html.
3. Select Create Topic.
4. Choose the most appropriate Forum Category.
5. Add the title, message, tags, images, or video.
6. Publish the topic.

FILTERING
---------
Click any category card to show only that section.
Use Show all categories to return to the complete forum.
Search works across titles, messages, categories, and tags.

EXISTING TOPICS
---------------
Older topics are preserved. The site automatically assigns them to a category
based on their title and tags, then saves the updated category to localStorage.

CUSTOMIZATION
-------------
Edit FORUM_CATEGORIES near the top of js/forums.js to rename categories,
change icons, or alter descriptions. Keep the category key synchronized with
the option values in forums.html.
