AetherCraft v2.8.2

- Moved Java/Bedrock AetherCraft JSON Blueprint Converter to blueprint-converter.html.
- Build Academy is now tutorial-focused.
- Expanded built-in tutorial catalog with additional castles, homes, interiors, infrastructure, survival, fantasy, Nether, redstone and community builds.
- Replaced generic material labels with named Minecraft block palettes such as Spruce Planks, Stripped Spruce Logs, Oak/Dark Oak/Cherry/Mangrove variants, Stone Bricks, Cobblestone, Deepslate Tiles, Blackstone and more.
- Material quantities scale per tutorial dimensions and complexity and are intended as the required planning list for the generated structural tutorial. Decorative substitutions may change totals.
- Existing Resource Pack Lab remains a separate page.
