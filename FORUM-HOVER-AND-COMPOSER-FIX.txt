AETHERCRAFT v2.3.1 - FORUM HOVER AND COMPOSER FIX
=================================================

PROFILE HOVER CARD
------------------
The profile preview now remains visible while the pointer moves from a forum
avatar or username onto the preview card. The View Profile button can be
clicked without the card disappearing or flickering.

CREATE TOPIC MODAL
------------------
The topic composer is attached directly to the page body, centered in the
browser viewport, and limited to the visible screen height. When the form is
taller than the screen, only the modal panel scrolls. The page behind it stays
locked in place.

CONTROLS
--------
- Click Create Topic to open the centered composer.
- Scroll inside the composer when necessary.
- Press Escape, click the X, or click the dark backdrop to close it.

CACHE NOTE
----------
After replacing an older version, press Ctrl + F5 once in the browser so the
updated forums.js and style.css files are loaded.
